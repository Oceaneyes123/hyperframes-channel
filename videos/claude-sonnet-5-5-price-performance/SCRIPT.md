# Draft script

One takeaway: Sonnet 5.5 brings unusually strong coding and everyday-work
capability at a lower cost than Opus 5.5, but the best choice still depends on
task difficulty and the value of deeper judgment.

Required information mapped to scenes: model choice and constraints (1–2),
speed and pricing (3–4), per-task efficiency (4), coding and knowledge-work
results (5–6), Opus's difficult-work advantage (7), qualified conclusion (8).

Out of scope: a universal model ranking, claims about subscription-plan value,
or an exhaustive competitor leaderboard.

Duration: approximately 100 seconds at a measured, conversational pace. This is
a planning estimate only; no narration has been generated or timed.
Captions: off. Sound cues: off. BGM: off.

## Line 1 — Hook
**Display:** Near the flagship?

    A flagship can do more. But how much of that extra capability do everyday tasks actually need?

## Line 2 — Setup / problem
**Display:** Capability. Time. Cost.

    Choosing isn't just picking the smartest model. Response time, repeated tokens, and retries push back on every real task.

## Line 3 — Speed
**Display:** 30%+ faster output*

    Anthropic says Sonnet 5.5 generates output more than thirty percent faster than Sonnet 5. That's generation speed, not a promise that every whole task finishes that much sooner.

## Line 4 — Price / per-task efficiency
**Display:** $2 in · $10 out / million tokens

    Those list rates match Sonnet 5 and are half of Opus 5.5's. Anthropic says fewer tokens can lower task cost by up to thirty percent. In CodeRabbit's 44-review run, Sonnet 5.5 model calls averaged forty-six cents per review versus a dollar sixteen for Sonnet 5; scoring was still pending.

## Line 5 — Coding capability
**Display:** CursorBench 4.0 · Max effort

    On CursorBench's multi-file coding tasks, Sonnet 5.5 scored fifty-five point five percent, up from Sonnet 5's thirty-four point one, and close to Opus at fifty-seven point eight.

## Line 6 — Knowledge work
**Display:** GDPval-AA · one evaluation

    On one knowledge-work test, Sonnet scored eighteen forty-four, just two points behind Opus. A close score here doesn't make the models interchangeable.

## Line 7 — Twist / limitation
**Display:** Hard reviews favor Opus

    CodeRabbit's thirteen hardest review cases show the other side: Opus caught eight known bugs at standard effort, to Sonnet's six, and ten at max. It's a small, specific test, but difficult judgment can justify the premium.

## Line 8 — Payoff
**Display:** Strong value. Not every job.

    So the case for Sonnet 5.5 is the combination: strong results on selected coding and knowledge-work tests, faster output, and lower operating cost than Opus. For well-scoped everyday work, that's a compelling mid-range value. For open-ended work where the hardest judgment matters, choose Opus.
