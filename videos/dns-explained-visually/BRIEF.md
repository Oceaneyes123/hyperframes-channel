## Intent

Teach a friendly, technically accurate visual walkthrough of what happens when a viewer types `google.com`: DNS locates the destination; the browser's website connection comes afterward.

## Customizations

- Reusable illustrated client, resolver, root, TLD, authoritative-server, cache, request-packet, response-packet, and connection-line language.
- Large high-contrast labels, dark neutral ground, restrained glow, and motion that reveals the lookup.

## Notes

- No avatars, talking heads, stock footage, fake terminals, DNSSEC, zone-file internals, or anycast detail.
- Explain the uncached lookup without suggesting every request reaches every layer or that a server role means one physical machine.
- Narration uses the repository Supertonic production path after storyboard approval; captions and BGM remain off.
