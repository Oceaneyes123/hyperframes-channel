## Scale and bounds

Primary visible artwork: gate 420px, device 380px, site 340px; overview is
asymmetric through OTHER. Packet hero enlarges to 420px in inspection views;
travelling packets are supporting objects. Values 40px, labels 36px, decisions
48px. Full value highlights have 18px padding. Use local Barlow Bold and IBM Plex
Mono Bold. Letter spacing 0. Do not scale font sizes with viewport; scale the
whole fixed-format storyboard canvas for phone inspection.

## Layers and handoffs

Back to front: canvas; routes/boundary; floor contact shadows; gate-posts;
packet travel wrapper; packet body (back/front/flap/seal); rigid evidence;
gate-arm occluding the crossing on block; ledger/rule details; decision mark.
The gate-badge's pre-drawn cross is omitted: a cross before checking would imply
the decision was already made. Use a neutral shield mark until allow/block resolves.
Contact shadows remain on the floor, tighten at landing, and do not travel with
the packet. No background idle motion is needed.

Travel, whole-body squash, hinged parts and rigid labels use separate wrappers.
Use local flow symbol layers. Keep content behind the opaque wrapper; reveal a
hazard only on the device's side after arrival. Refusal and log records are separate
copies, never the dropped packet returning to its sender.

Scene 1 is an explicit outcome hook; scene 2 rewinds to the connection's start.
Scenes 2-4 hold the new attempt at the decision; scene 5 is an isolated fresh-attempt
order counterexample. Restore rule order and main attempt state at its end.
Scene 6 inherits that main attempt, allows it and records state; it then advances
connection progress and returns the reply. Scenes 7-8 retain the record and boundary.
Scene 9 compares capabilities at that same boundary. Scene 10 follows a later
allowed web payload. Scene 11 starts a fresh app attempt; scene 12 reprises the
two decisions. Match-cut endpoints unless STORYBOARD.md names the time jump.
