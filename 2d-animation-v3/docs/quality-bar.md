# V3 quality bar from user corrections

Current authoring/review direction from the November 5, 2026 gift-card project. Explicit project instructions may override creative defaults; record overrides and preserve accepted artifacts. This guidance is not an activated CQ rule, automatic detector or quality guarantee.

## Narration: choose clarity by listening

- Default to local VoiceStudio; never use Fish TTS or fall back after failure. Installation, a successful WAV or a familiar preset is not acceptance. Audition hook, demanding explanation and payoff for clear pronunciation, complete endings, natural connected delivery and consistent identity. Never accelerate speech to fit a plan.
- Delegate creation only to a dedicated `gpt-6-luna` subagent: generation, recovery and generated-audio validation. Writing, voice selection, word alignment, visuals, video rendering and approval stay outside its scope. Reuse matching active jobs/checkpoints; never duplicate synthesis.
- Follow exact engine/model/voice/settings/licence records in the [audio reference](../../2d-animation/channel/AUDIO_VERSIONS.md). Accepted gift-card OmniVoice audio is private non-commercial evaluation, not a commercially cleared channel default. Kitten presets remain unaccepted candidates.
- Generate the complete accepted script; preserve raw/normalized WAVs and hashes. Review the full take and every line boundary. ASR differences flag listening checks, neither prove mispronunciation nor authorize rewriting. Audition approval does not approve the full take. Import timing only after the user's full listening/timing review; derive scene lengths from that recording. See [alignment/audio](local-alignment-and-audio.md).

## Backgrounds: visibly tactile, composed and quiet behind copy

New tactile scenes use `rough-paper@1`: coarse fibers, fixed ink flecks and irregular edges visible at 360px width. Faint grain alone is insufficient. Keep noise seeded, static and below foreground text; no boiling texture/distracting animation. Preserve existing `paper-grain@1`.

Use tonal planes, depth, editorial framing, contextual geometry or technical grids. Keep connected palettes coherent; change environments with viewpoint. Texture/color swaps alone do not distinguish scenes. Preserve a clear focal silhouette and quiet reading area.

## Typography: compose each label for its scene

- Vary object-relative placement, scale, alignment, spacing and emphasis. Avoid repeated full-width header bands, identical boxes or fixed top placement. Select freestanding bold type, marker highlights, drawn accents, offset stamps, ribbons or badges for hierarchy/meaning, never empty-space decoration.
- Keep at most two essential short labels, normally 1–3 words. Exact commands, numbers and qualifications may need more. Narration carries explanation; avoid transcripts, paragraphs and label walls. Text stays editable/renderer-supported; store decoration in canonical drawings and attach it during movement.
- Apply eligible treatments throughout, including later scenes. Use all ten library styles where existing copy, meaning and timing fit; reuse suitable styles without adding copy for quotas. Settle root entrances first. Preserve padding, contrast, reading time and deliberate stable continuity labels. Inspect frame zero, treatment and settled state: no empty unexplained banner or hidden condition. Dynamic design need not mean constant text motion.

## Motion: a decisive mechanism, not a wobbling object

Before effects, specify initial relationship, causal action, changed state and receiver response. Persistent objects assemble, fan, gather, split, route, replace, unfold, transform or hand off information. Give actions readable preparation, short decisive execution, clear landing and stable payoff. Where a receiver exists it visibly responds; unrelated pulses do not count.

Reject small nudges, shaking, jitter, cosmetic pulses, endless loops, camera drift, entrances alone and effect counts as substitutes. Ten frames alone do not make motion punchy: distance, silhouette, pose, acceleration and consequence must read at phone size. Squash, overshoot/impact reinforce the action; settle once instead of repeatedly returning an unchanged item to its pose.

Intense defaults at 30fps: 6–12-frame entrances, 8–18-frame focused actions, meaningful development roughly each second where teaching permits. Respect preset minima. Reading holds, accurate relationships and measured narration override cadence. Never add filler, speed speech, pad runtime or invent events to clear warnings.

Root actors may travel between authored positions through supported actions. Fixed parts retain registered geometry/containment; this does not restrict roots to tiny movements. Inspection punch-ins clarify results after object-led action; camera motion cannot supply the mechanism.

Vary choreography by meaning; do not repeat one launch/squash routine. Show the main object early in the hook and preserve identity through handoffs/factual distinctions. For gift cards, aggregate accounting estimates must not resemble deductions from customer balances; fees, expiry, breakage and government remittance stay distinct.

## Review ladder: stop before repeating a rejected style at scale

1. Review concept/storyboard endpoints at phone size: hierarchy, texture, labels and correct relationships. Static approval/contact sheets cannot establish motion energy or audio quality.
2. Animate one representative mechanism with exact narration at normal speed. Identify cause, state change and receiver response. If it merely moves slightly or shakes, revise. Obtain user approval before extending the direction.
3. Review all scenes with/without sound: reading time, role changes, conditions, first/middle/final states and every seam. Inspect actual compositing/silhouettes; matched geometry can still hide a foreground object behind the next background.
4. Preserve the approved sample. Check exact source, audio, timing and shot-payload hashes before carrying decisions forward. Never label changed/unseen footage watched; sample approval covers only that sample.
5. Record full-sequence approval separately, then existing production gates and explicit local-render authorization. Decode/frame/seek/audio checks establish integrity, not creative acceptance, retention, rights or publishing authorization.

Inspectors remain advisory for creative judgment; they cannot detect every bland background, false causal relationship or recurrence. Never weaken structural checks, frozen hashes or export thresholds for clean reports.
