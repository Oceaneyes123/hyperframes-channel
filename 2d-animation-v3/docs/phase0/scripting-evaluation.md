## Specific compatibility findings

The installed hook skill seeks audience relevance and story resolution. Its
attention formulas can also encourage manufactured stakes if used mechanically.
V3's facts and viewer outcome must precede hook selection; sample money figures
remain explicitly hypothetical and never establish income or demand.

The installed humanizer requires supported facts to survive editing and forbids
invented details. Keep it after drafting, then verify claim preservation. A
successful language-cleanup pass is not editorial acceptance.

The reviewed [copy-editing source](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/blob/e85f3cbc2e4a79bf33b2a12e9bdd9087d414735b/skills/copy-editing/SKILL.md)
specializes in marketing material and includes a multi-agent review approach.
Use only the scoped editorial method in a controlled trial. This task forbids
subagents, so none of that procedure was executed or presented as independent review.

The reviewed [video-hook-writer source](https://github.com/SkillMedev/creator-studio/blob/21980fd85666053f4385fbf254bf960f03d34279/skills/video-hook-writer/SKILL.md)
requires an overlay and restricts spoken hooks to a fixed short length. Those
constraints can conflict with minimal overlays and complete beginner explanations.
Its platform-ranking claims were not independently substantiated in this review.
Do not import example results or statistics as channel facts.

## Proposed controlled comparison

Use the same five briefs, audience, supplied facts, approximate length, model
version/settings, and source freeze for each condition. Record all actual values.

- **Baseline:** original v2 workflow from the frozen files and installed skill pins.
- **Trial:** same research and draft inputs, adding scoped editorial passes.
- **Optional ablation:** vary only hook-development method on the same completed
  draft, so a hook trial does not silently become a whole-script rewrite.

Existing v2 drafts cover three topics. The two AI/business briefs do not yet have
matched frozen v2 outputs; generate both conditions from their identical briefs
when that comparison is scoped. Do not manufacture missing historical drafts.

Blind labels/order for human reviewers, keeping provenance separately. Reject
factual errors, unsupported claims and broken hook/payoff promises before style
preference. Then record natural read-aloud quality, clarity of the mechanism,
specific weak lines and preference/tie with reasons. Preserve disagreements.
Use multiple independent people when available; one author's critique is labeled
as such. AI ratings are diagnostic notes, never proof of human preference.

The [five-brief packet](scripting-briefs.json) is ready. Draft generation, blinded
human review and efficacy results are **not performed**. Do not report a winning
skill, retention uplift or more human-sounding output until that evidence exists.
