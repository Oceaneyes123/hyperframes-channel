## Proposed narration

You could start a DeepSeek V4.1 Flash business without building an app. Here's a service you can build, and how to pitch it to a business owner.

Start with a salon. Ask the owner which booking questions they keep answering, and where replies get stuck.

Build a demo around those questions, using made-up inquiries and the owner's approved FAQ.

A customer submits a form. Their question lands in Google Sheets, and n8n picks up the new row.

It sends the question and FAQ to DeepSeek. Use the model name deepseek-flash, and request a category and reply draft as JSON: structured fields your workflow can read.

Check those fields, update the sheet, and notify the owner. They check the facts, edit, and approve before sending. Missing information goes back to them, too.

Test a booking question and one the FAQ cannot answer.

Then show the owner that workflow. Ask whether it fits their process, and offer setup, testing, and a handover, with optional maintenance.

Agree who pays the API and hosting bills. DeepSeek charges by usage.

You're selling a working inquiry process, with the owner in control of every reply.
