# Script proposal — Your Payment Failed. The Money Still Left.

Status: accepted by the user on 2026-10-27 with the pizza hook below. Timing is an estimate, not recorded audio.

## Hook candidates
1. You order a pizza. The payment failed, so why is money missing from your bank account? **Recommended:** shows the contradiction immediately.
2. Your payment failed. So why is your money missing?
3. The screen says failed. Your bank says something else.

## Proposed narration (~65 seconds, estimated)

**0–4 sec**
You order a pizza. The payment failed, so why is money missing from your bank account?

**4–15 sec**
Here’s the mystery: the app’s message isn’t the whole payment. Your bank, the payment network, and the merchant all have to exchange information. If a response times out, the screen can say failed while the real transaction status is still unclear.

**15–29 sec**
Sometimes the bank has only reserved the money. That’s a hold: your available balance drops, but the payment hasn’t necessarily been completed. Other times a transfer is still being checked, or a charge has actually posted.

**29–44 sec**
Those paths don’t all resolve the same way. A hold can be released. A failed transfer may need to be returned or reconciled. A posted charge may need an investigation or refund. How long it takes depends on the provider and payment method.

**44–65 sec**
So check whether the transaction is pending or posted. Save the amount, date, and reference number if you have it: they help the provider find the record. Contact your bank or payment provider if it doesn’t clear, or immediately if you don’t recognize the charge. A failed screen is a clue—not the final answer.

## Editorial review

The hook introduces a visible discrepancy. The explanation keeps a timeout's outcome unknown and distinguishes a held amount from a posted charge. The three resolution paths are alternatives, not guaranteed outcomes. The final action uses a reference as helpful, not mandatory. Duration and delivery require an actual audition; the current estimate is provisional.
