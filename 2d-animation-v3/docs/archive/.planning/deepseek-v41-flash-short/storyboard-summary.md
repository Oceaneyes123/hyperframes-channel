# Owner control desk storyboard

Estimated 70 seconds. Gateway base with disclosed Codex-authored refinement. No acceptance.

## Shot 1 — 8 seconds

Promise a useful inquiry service without an app; establish the owner's release boundary.

Move the inquiry from intake into a draft beneath the closed gate. Stop there; imply neither approval nor delivery.

## Shot 2 — 10 seconds

Ground the salon demo in repeated questions, reply bottlenecks and an owner-approved FAQ.

First move a question toward the gate and stop at the bottleneck. Replace it with made-up inquiries; bring the approved FAQ alongside. No approval gesture.

## Shot 3 — 7 seconds

Expose the intake mechanism: form submission becomes a sheet row, then triggers n8n.

The form hands off its question into the highlighted Sheets row; that row triggers the connected n8n nodes. Hold the resulting two software stations.

## Shot 4 — 11 seconds

Explain question-plus-FAQ model input and machine-readable category and reply-draft output.

Hand the question and approved FAQ to the DeepSeek chip. Reveal category and reply-draft documents as separate JSON fields; hold the exact model identifier.

## Shot 5 — 10 seconds

Separate field validation from owner fact-checking, editing and explicit release.

Validate JSON fields and update the same Sheets row. Notification draws owner attention. Owner checks facts, edits and explicitly approves; only that human action lifts the gate. Missing information returns to the owner. No automatic send.

## Shot 6 — 6 seconds

Test both an answerable booking inquiry and an inquiry outside the FAQ.

Compare a booking test with a question absent from the FAQ. One produces a reviewable draft; the other requests owner input. Both routes remain under owner control.

## Shot 7 — 18 seconds

Pitch process fit and service scope, assign running-cost responsibility, and restate owner control.

Present the built workflow and setup/testing/handover offer; keep maintenance optional. Use the cloud and document as hosting/API cost objects while narration assigns responsibility. Return the pending inquiry to the central review desk. No invented amounts or sale.
