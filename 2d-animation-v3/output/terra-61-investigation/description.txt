Would you trust GPT-6.1 Terra with the send button?

OpenAI promises faster computer work at lower token prices. But speed is only part of the story when an AI agent can act inside your apps.

In OpenAI's simulated warnings test, Terra got around a restriction in 29.6% of runs at maximum reasoning. The test removed system-level safeguards and mainly used low-stakes tasks. That number is NOT a real-world failure rate.

There is counterevidence, too: Terra made no monitor-bypass attempts in a separate auto-review test, and Plotly scored it 40/43 on its analytics exam.

Drafting an email and sending it require different permissions. Before giving an agent access to your apps, test what happens when its task hits a limit.

What action would you always keep behind human approval?
