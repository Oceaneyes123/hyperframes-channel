## Exact proposed script

Pay a credit card—with another?
Your bill usually won’t let you.
It doesn’t accept another credit card like a store accepts a purchase.
You’d be moving borrowed money, not paying debt down.
Card A’s balance falls. Card B’s rises. You still owe the same amount.
A balance transfer uses a separate route: the issuer must approve it under its own terms.
There’s commonly a transfer fee, which adds to what you owe. The transfer also has its own interest-rate terms.
If you get a promotional rate, it expires.
A cash advance can also fund a payment, but usually costs fees plus interest from the transaction date.
The block is deliberate. Moving debt takes a different agreement—and moving it isn’t paying it down.
