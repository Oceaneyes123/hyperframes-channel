Why can you buy things with a credit card, but usually can’t use it to pay another credit card’s bill?

You can use that card at a store, but card companies generally won’t accept it as a bill payment.

You can sometimes move the balance, though. That’s a balance transfer, and the company taking on the debt has to approve it.

Move the whole balance from this card to this one, and the first card is paid off. You still owe the money on the second.

The transfer has its own interest rate and usually a fee, which adds to your debt. If there’s a promotional rate, check when it ends.

A cash advance can also cover a payment, but usually comes with fees and interest starting that day.

So the first card can be paid off while you still owe just as much, or more, elsewhere.
