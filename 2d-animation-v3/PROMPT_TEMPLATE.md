# V3 explainer prompt

Copy this for new Studio V3 explainers. V1/V2 retain their own workflows.

```text
Create a faceless explainer using the current V3 workflow.

Topic: [TOPIC]
Audience: [AUDIENCE, or beginners]
Single takeaway: [TAKEAWAY, or propose one]
Required information and sources: [REQUIREMENTS / SOURCES, or research primary sources]
Duration: [LIMIT, or natural pace covering the approved explanation]
Format: portrait 9:16, 1080 x 1920, 30fps unless overridden
Narration: clear local VoiceStudio voice, natural speed; no Fish fallback
Motion: Intense, meaningful object-led choreography and readable holds
Music / sound effects / captions: off unless requested

Read AGENTS.md, docs/STATUS.md and docs/architecture.md. Apply docs/quality-bar.md
before authoring. Use docs/reference-map.md to load only the current stage's guide
and selected capability sections. Follow existing V3 stores, contracts and gates;
preserve unrelated edits and immutable source/approval records.

Preserve exact sourced claims, qualifications and approved narration. Show
mechanisms with large recognizable objects and at most two essential short
labels. Use visibly rough static rough-paper@1 backgrounds with layered depth
and quiet reading areas. Compose text per scene: vary placement, scale,
alignment and selective accents; avoid uniform header bands/boxed labels.
Store decoration in canonical drawings and attach it to moving text.

Plan relationship, cause, changed state and receiver response before presets.
Use decisive assembly, transfer, transformation and handoff; avoid small nudges,
shaking, cosmetic pulses and camera drift as substitutes. Vary choreography by
meaning. Target 6–12-frame entrances and 8–18-frame actions at 30fps, respecting
preset minima and reading holds. Never speed narration, pad runtime or invent
events for motion quotas. Use all ten text styles throughout where existing
copy, meaning and timing fit, including later scenes; reuse without adding copy.

Accept the exact script before full narration. Use a dedicated gpt-6-luna
subagent only for audio generation, recovery and validation. The coordinator/user
retains writing, voice choice, alignment, visuals and approval; video rendering
has its separate assignment. Reuse matching active jobs/checkpoints. Audition
the hook, demanding explanation and payoff; review the complete take and every
line boundary before timing import or locking scene lengths. Installation or a
valid WAV is not voice acceptance. Preserve accepted audio, exact model/voice
licence scope and D-drive storage; never silently switch engines/presets/providers.

Concept selection, exact storyboard acceptance, full narration/timing review and
motion review are separate. Prepare concrete reviewable work; reuse unchanged
approvals without asking again. Review concepts/storyboard, then one exact narrated
representative mechanism at normal speed and phone size. Obtain approval before
extending its direction. Inspect seams, continuity, layering and occlusion.
Review the full sequence with/without sound; sample approval does not cover
unseen scenes. Model advice, stills and diagnostics cannot grant human approval.

Render the final only after full review, current production gates and explicit
render authorization. Run existing decode, frame/seek and audio verification.
Report blockers without weakening checks. Never commit, push, upload or publish
without separate authorization. Private narration acceptance does not clear
commercial use.

For requested packaging, use a portrait Shorts thumbnail with a recognizable
subject and short accurate hook. Save the selected image, prompt, title,
description and tags in the project; claims must not exceed the script.
```
